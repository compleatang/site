After reading so much AI drivel my eyes were about to fall out, I have decided it was time to write again. This blog will be a chance for me to write again which is a skill and habit that has been sorely lacking from my habits these last years. And it's something that I now quite miss doing.

I think that we as humans are going to increasingly understand writing that has been crafted by humans and that which has been crafted by machines. I already have a few turn of phrases that just make me want to throw all the GPUs into the pits of volcanic hell because they're more overused than a summer pop song from my youth. This is my space to avoid that and to produce something authentically human into the world. Although I do not claim that the styling and site management itself will not be deeply robot managed. But the content and writing will be all me (a human).

---

For the last dozen years I've been trying to startup. The next entries in this blog will be about many of the things that I've learned along that journey. Mostly about all the ways that I fucked up and failed to actually, well, startup. It's about failure. And my increasingly toxic relationship with that word. 

In the holidays of 2013 I went home to the US and was spending time with my family. As I had been in a bit of transition phase at the time, my father asked me what I really wanted to do "with my life" -- as father's are wont to do. I told him that I liked being a lawyer but what I really wanted to be doing was to build software. I think I said "run a software company" rather than "build software" two notions I learned over the years are quite different. And that was the start of an unlikely journey into this world of entrepreneurship.

Since that time I've had an astounding record of misses. Or near misses. Or learnings. Or, just failures.

Just an utterly amazing record. Let's dive in.
